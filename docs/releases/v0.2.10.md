*veles 0.2.10, 2026-11-21.*

The phone can now stay asleep when its gateway is idle, and `/new` on a
chat surface keeps the new session attached to the same conversation.

## Fixes

### The phone sleeps instead of running a permanent wake/restart loop

- A desktop left open polled `/api/fleet/alerts` every 30 seconds without
  the passive marker used by its other timers. Each poll refreshed the
  phone's idle clock, so an unattended phone could not idle-exit. The poll
  now carries `X-Veles-Passive: 1`; requests from actual desktop use still
  count as activity.
- The phone's idle stop also stops its userland. Previously, the gateway's
  idle exit stopped its service but only unbound the Alpine userland. That
  binding had started the userland's sticky foreground service, which kept
  its broker and partial wake lock alive indefinitely.
  On an SM-S942B the userland was still awake more than a day after the
  gateway had last been used.
- A userland started by that binding now stops when the last gateway
  binding goes away. Its broker and wake lock go with it. An explicit
  Start in Veles userland, or opening that app, still keeps it running;
  an idle gateway does not cancel that choice.
- A pool-member phone used to schedule its next liveness wake only one
  minute after its idle exit. It ran for five minutes, slept for one,
  and started again, indefinitely. The liveness interval is now 15
  minutes. Cron jobs and notifications matching a trigger still wake the
  phone at their own times.
- Between those reports, an unreachable ephemeral phone may appear stale
  in the pool. Placement skips it while it is stale rather than keeping
  it awake merely to appear available.

### `/new` keeps the session in its channel

- On IRC and other chat surfaces, `/new` switched to a fresh transcript
  but left the new session's chat address and caller identity attached
  only to the old session id. The fresh session disappeared from `/join`,
  and a later background wake had no chat to answer into.
- The new session now retains the channel, network route and caller
  identity. `/resume` keeps the resumed session's title too.
- A `/resume` naming a session currently held by a different chat sender
  is refused before any binding or address is changed. Resuming an
  offline session still works as before.
