*veles 0.2.8, 2026-11-17.*

Session mail no longer waits for the end of a turn, a session can fetch
and answer its own mail, and a Stop on the desktop no longer takes the
mail queued behind it down with it.

## Changes

### Mail reaches a busy session at its next tool result

- Until now, mail to a session only ran when the session was between
  turns. A session busy with a long turn left its mail waiting until the
  turn ended, sometimes for hours, and the only way to get it in sooner
  was to stop the turn.
- Now a running turn takes in waiting mail after each tool result, when
  the sender has at least the rights the turn runs with. The mail enters
  with a line naming its sender, saying that another agent or program
  wrote it rather than the user, and how to answer it.
- Mail from a sender with fewer rights still waits for a turn of its
  own, which runs with the lower of the two rights as before. The
  running turn is told only that it waits (its id, the sending session
  and its size), never its text.
- `agent.injections.mail = false` turns this off; mail then waits for
  the end of the turn, as in 0.2.7.

### The `sessions` tool: inbox and reply

- `inbox` fetches the session's own mail: it takes in what may enter the
  running turn, lists what waits for a turn of its own, and shows the
  mail answered recently.
- `reply` answers a mail the running turn took in. The first answer
  wins. A mail the turn took in and did not answer gets the turn's final
  answer as its reply.
- A turn that is stopped or fails puts the mail it took in and did not
  answer back in the queue. Mail that a turn left half done when its
  process died is marked failed at the end of the next turn, with a note
  to send it again.

## Fixes

### Stop on the desktop ends only the turn it stops

- The desktop runs queued turns one after another. It gave all of them
  the cancel signal of the turn that started the queue, so after a Stop
  every queued turn behind it, mail included, failed at once with "the
  turn was interrupted before it finished" without running. Each queued
  turn now gets its own.
